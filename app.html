Psyche

A self-knowledge and self-observation app for iPhone

  • Psychological questionnairesValidated scales such as GAD-7 for anxiety, with results explained in plain language.
  • 14-day self-observationShort daily notes on your state and its triggers, so you can see what affects you.
  • History and PDF reportSave your results, track changes, and share a report with a professional if you choose.
  • PrivacyNo account, no ads, no analytics. Your data stays on your iPhone.
  • Full VersionUnlocks in-depth tests. The core questionnaires are free.
